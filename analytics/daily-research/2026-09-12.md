### 32 · Single-window referent clause

An Amendment 2 referent clause that says **where a screened term's occurrences lie**, written from an instrument that can only return the **first** match. The defect is invisible because the clause is true of what was seen and unverifiable as to the rest, and it is corrosive precisely where Amendment 2 is meant to bite — a nonzero count annotated with a confident referent reads as *excluded*, which is the mirror of reading it as *corroboration*.
