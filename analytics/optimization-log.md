# Optimization log — system & method improvement ideas

The full council raises **at most one genuinely new idea per daily meeting** to improve or
optimize the system, the method, the fingerprint, or the search strategy toward answering the
research question or finding a breakthrough. Each is a dated bullet; the council dedups against
this list before adding. This is the running idea bank — ideas move to a PR/roadmap when adopted.

## Ideas (newest first)
- 2026-07-08 — State-Respondent Post-Award Challenge Monitor: add a periodic search track targeting post-award judicial proceedings for cases already in the project record — specifically: (a) set-aside applications at the seat of arbitration (Swiss Bundesgericht for PCA-Geneva cases; Svea Court / Swedish SC for SCC cases; English High Court for LCIA/ICC-London cases); (b) enforcement challenges at key enforcement courts; (c) parallel domestic proceedings (criminal or civil) that state-respondents invoke as grounds for post-award jurisdictional challenges. Triggered by today's discovery: China's two Swiss FSC set-aside applications against the Jason Yu Song v. China award (4A_46/2024, dismissed April 17, 2025; 4A_528/2024, dismissed June 26, 2025) were in the public record for over a year but absent from the project record. The second challenge used a Chinese criminal court judgment arguing illegal investment — a post-award jurisdictional attack on the only publicly known ISDS award against China. Implementation: quarterly targeted searches of the Swiss Bundesgericht public search (bger.ch) and domstol.se for Swedish SC, keyed to case names and parties from the project's insight ledger. Distinguishes from the Tagged-Case Posture Tracker (July 3) which targets UNCTAD Navigator, italaw, and ICSID case pages — national court post-award challenge proceedings for PCA/SCC cases do not appear reliably on those databases. Effort: low. _Status: proposed._
- 2026-07-07 — Content-type metadata tag for IAReporter "Looking Back" articles: add a `content_type` metadata field to the classifier output with values including `new_filing`, `new_ruling`, `retrospective_review`, `commentary`, and `regulatory`. When the source is IAReporter Headlines and the title begins with "Looking Back:", assign `content_type: retrospective_review` and apply a relevance score cap of 30. Triggered by today's digest item — the first IAReporter "Looking Back" retrospective (Suez v. Argentina (1), ICSID ARB/03/17, 2006) to appear in the digest. The current system correctly scored it at 25 (below threshold), but the retrospective format was not immediately distinguishable from a new-ruling item by title alone. Impact: (1) Allows analytics officer to distinguish new-development from retrospective-survey receptivity in per-source yield analysis; (2) Enables the chairman to calibrate backsource work — `retrospective_review` items are contextual rather than new developments; (3) Prevents historical retrospectives from ever reaching the Attention threshold (≥ 40). Extend to other known retrospective/review series as they appear. Effort: low. _Status: proposed._
- 2026-07-06 — Ring 2 classifier: require state-as-measure-taker. Add a mandatory sub-criterion to the Ring 2 "judicial or regulatory measure" definition in `fingerprint.yaml`: the measure must be taken by or attributable to a state or state body acting in its public capacity within a treaty or investment law framework — not a domestic court adjudicating a private commercial dispute between private parties. Triggered by today's backsource of digest item 2 (June 29): the classifier scored the Court of Appeal of Santiago's set-aside of the Joyvio/Australis commercial arbitration award at Ring 2 (score 25, WATCH) because a court issued a decision affecting an award — but Joyvio/Australis is a private commercial dispute with no BIT, no state party, no IP-as-investment nexus. The "judicial measure" Ring 2 concept in the research question requires a state act challenging an investor's treaty-protected rights. Implementation: add `measure_taker: state_or_state_body_in_public_capacity` as a required Ring 2 sub-criterion in `fingerprint.yaml`, with Joyvio/Australis as a labeled false-positive exemplar and Einarsson (Canada petroleum disclosure regime) as a labeled true-positive exemplar. Impact: eliminates the false-positive class of domestic commercial arbitration set-aside decisions; reduces low-relevance IAReporter noise. Effort: low. _Status: proposed._
- 2026-07-05 — Pre-filing BIT dispute notice monitor: add a periodic search track targeting Chinese stock exchange disclosures, IAReporter notices-of-intent/arbitration coverage, and company announcement databases (EDGAR 8-K, RNS/Regulatory News Service) for IP/technology/pharmaceutical BIT dispute notices filed under Chinese BITs (or by Chinese investors against European respondent states) during the pre-ICSID-registration cooling-off period. Triggered by the Wingtech/Nexperia v. Netherlands discovery (formal notice October 2025; found July 5, 2026 — 9-month lag): a Chinese investor filed a USD 8 billion BIT dispute notice over IP/technology assets in October 2025; the Kluwer Arbitration Blog analyzed it in January 2026; the project found it only in July 2026 because the digest's scoring architecture tracks registered ICSID cases, not pre-filing notices. Distinguishes from: tagged-case posture tracker (tracks *registered* cases in the project record); full-text intake source (credentialed ISDS dockets — post-registration); host-state domestic law monitor (regulatory changes, not dispute notices). This addresses the pre-registration intake gap. Effort: low (monthly searches of IAReporter notices, Chinese stock exchange filings on IP/BIT disputes). _Status: proposed._
- 2026-07-04 — Host-state domestic law monitor: add a dedicated periodic search track targeting regulatory developments in key BIT host states (China, India, Brazil) for domestic law changes that define, protect, or restrict trade secrets, pharmaceutical data, and data assets in ways that affect the Ring 1 covered-investment characterization and Ring 2 triggering-measure analysis. Triggered by today's SAMR Order No. 126 finding (issued February 24, 2026; surfaced by the project July 4, 2026 — a 4+ month lag): China's most substantial trade-secret reform in 30 years, explicitly covering "data," was not in the project record until a proactive T04 search today. Distinguishes from existing ideas: tagged-case posture tracker follows ISDS case progression; full-text intake source covers ISDS dockets; cross-model verifier addresses quality. This addresses a structural intake blind spot — the project monitors what ISDS tribunals say about trade secrets, but not what key host states are doing domestically to define and protect the very assets at issue. Implementation: 4–6 targeted searches per month covering SAMR, NMPA, MOFCOM (China), DPIIT, MoHFW (India), ANVISA, INPI (Brazil) plus EU pharmaceutical regulatory developments. Effort: low. _Status: proposed._
- 2026-07-03 — Tagged-case posture tracker: add a periodic docket-watch module that queries UNCTAD Navigator, italaw, and ICSID case pages for cases already in the project record (tagged by ring assignment and case identifier in the insight ledger), surfacing new documents or proceedings to the chairman's agenda when the case progresses to a new institutional or judicial layer (e.g., annulment proceeding, appeal filing, Supreme Court ruling). Addresses the temporal-depth gap: in *Okuashvili v. Georgia*, the Svea Court of Appeal ruling (November 2024) and the Swedish Supreme Court leave-grant (September 2025) were not surfaced during the nine months between those events and the final Supreme Court ruling (June 2026). Distinguishes from existing ideas: cross-model verifier addresses *quality*; full-text intake addresses *breadth*; agent-SDK subagents address *architecture*. This addresses *temporal depth* — following known cases through their full institutional and judicial lifecycle, not just at first intake. _Status: proposed._
- 2026-07-02 — MFN-scope tag in Ring 3 fingerprint: add `mfn_dispute_resolution_scope` as a dedicated Ring 3 classifier tag in `fingerprint.yaml` to surface cases where an MFN clause is applied to dispute-resolution provisions (the Okuashvili mechanism). Currently the Ring 3 fingerprint captures `treaty_shopping`, `abuse_of_right`, and `critical_date` but does not specifically flag MFN-forum-access as a distinct mechanism. Adding the tag would: (a) surface future cases combining MFN-scope forum import with an IP investment claim at a higher Ring 3 relevance score; (b) flag Chinese BIT cases invoking this mechanism, providing a monitoring signal if doctrine shifts. Triggered by Okuashvili v. Georgia (Swedish SC, June 2026); confirmed closed for China BITs by July 2 session. Effort: low. _Status: proposed._
- 2026-07-01 — Targeted quoted-phrase search as substitute for 403-blocked PDFs: when a PDF is consistently inaccessible (UNCITRAL site, ICSID, italaw), construct search queries combining the document symbol (e.g., "A/CN.9/1246"), known subject-matter terms, and hypothesized specific language to trigger search-engine snippets that may include verbatim or near-verbatim document language. Today's example: querying "A/CN.9/1246 supplementary provisions scope application temporal 1 April 2027" returned a snippet with language appearing to reproduce document phrasing ("they could apply, for example, to treaties concluded on or after 1 April 2027"; "may need to be developed"), yielding a medium-confidence characterisation of the scope provision without direct PDF access. This technique is immediately actionable and complements (without substituting for) the UN ODS direct-retrieval proposal from June 30. _Status: proposed; demonstrated successfully today._
- 2026-06-30 — UN ODS as alternative UNCITRAL document retrieval route: UNCITRAL PDF links at uncitral.un.org/sites/default/files/ are consistently 403-blocked across sessions (A/CN.9/1246, session agenda PDF, and others). UN Commission documents (A/CN.9/... series) are submitted to the General Assembly and archived in the UN Official Document System (https://documents.un.org), which provides free public access to UN official documents. Searching the document symbol "A/CN.9/1246" in the UN ODS may successfully retrieve documents otherwise inaccessible via UNCITRAL's own site. This would directly resolve the open thread on A/CN.9/1246 temporal scope (non-retroactivity confirmation) and the broader UNCITRAL document access bottleneck. _Status: proposed._
