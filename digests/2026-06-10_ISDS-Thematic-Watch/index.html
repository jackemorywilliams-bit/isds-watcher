Telefónica challenged Colombia's reversion of telecommunications assets under a BIT, alleging breaches of fair and equitable treatment stemming from legislative changes and a Constitutional Court judgment. This case concerns a breach-of-contract dispute between a solar company and Lesotho over a renewable energy supply agreement, resolved through commercial arbitration and subsequently reviewed by South African courts on enforcement and validity grounds.
