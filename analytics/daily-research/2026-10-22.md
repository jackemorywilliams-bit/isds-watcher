### 3.5 THE DAY'S SUBSTANTIVE FINDING — the absence claim no longer needs continuous coverage

Row 6 `Procedural Order No. 11` returned, on today's state:

> ` organizational meeting with the parties by video conference. February 21, 2025 The Tribunal issues Procedural Order No. 11 concerning the organization of the hearing.&nbsp; March 3, 2025 - March 13, 2025 The Tribunal holds a hearing on jurisdiction and the merits in person in Calgary and by video conference. March 14, 2025 The Tribunal issues Procedural Order No. 12 concerning procedural matters.`

That is **chronology** text, not list text. Under the ordering property — `excerpt_of` returns
the **first** occurrence in visible prose, and the list region precedes the chronology region —
a first occurrence in the chronology means **the literal `Procedural Order No. 11` does not
occur anywhere in the list region of this page state.** Not in the observed windows, and **not
in Gap A′ or Gap B′ either**, because a hit inside a gap would have been the first occurrence
and would have been returned instead.

**This is what cures `1021-C10` for No. 11, and it cures it on a single page state rather than
on a composite of eight.** It cost one row. The four window rows corroborate it and bought
genuine new list text, but they are not what carries it. The cheaper instrument was the
ordering property, and I did not see that when I wrote the batch — I wrote four rows for a job
one row did.

**What this finding does NOT establish, stated so no successor over-reads it.**

1. **It is about No. 11 only.** `Procedural Order No. 12`'s list-absence still rests on
   yesterday's state (`770d97f5…`), where its row also returned chronology text. It was not
   re-fired today.
2. **It is about this literal only.** An entry worded `Procedural Order 11`, `PO-11`, or
   `Procedural Order No. 11 (Public Version)` with different intervening characters would not
   necessarily fire this find; the first two would not. The claim is about the string, not about
   the document.
3. **It rests on the derived ordering property**, not on an axiom. Today's rows tighten that
   derivation — row 1 shows the list opening immediately after the docket-summary fields, row 5
   shows the list running into the dated-document section, and row 6's chronology window sits
   near the page tail — but it remains an inference from observed window positions, and if the
   page ever reordered its regions the inference would fail silently.
4. **It is not a statement about the matter.** It supports only that the docket's published-
   decisions list does not name such an entry on the state observed today. **It is never that
   no such procedural order issued, and never that one is unpublished** — the project's absence
   rule is "not found in accessible sources". G16 bars this surface from founding a disposition;
   G10 bars reading a document designation as a tribunal's characterisation. The chronology's
   own words — a hearing "on jurisdiction and the merits", an order "concerning the organization
   of the hearing" — support **only that the docket so records them**, never that the tribunal
   bifurcated, joined, or decided anything.
