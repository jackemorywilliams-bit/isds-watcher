
The newest record was `2026-11-06.md`, committed `2026-11-06T22:31:27+00:00` — roughly **26.5
hours** before that line was printed. The watchdog read its age as **~0.0h**. The cause is at
`.github/workflows/heartbeat.yml:87-88`, which computes staleness from `os.path.getmtime`: git

and `age_h` is approximately zero on every run. **`stale = age_h > 36` can never be true.**

**The officer narrowed this and the narrowed form is the one that survives, so it is the one I
publish.** My draft said "the switch cannot fire at any staleness," which reads as *the workflow
cannot alert* — and it can. On its committed configuration — `runs-on: ubuntu-latest`,
`actions/checkout@v4`, on both the schedule and `workflow_dispatch` triggers — `age_h` is the age
of the checkout, **so the staleness limb cannot fire at any record age.** The workflow's only
reachable alert on this step is the zero-records limb at `:91-93`, which sets `stale = True` when
the glob returns nothing at all — and that is *absence*, not staleness. The officer also named a
suppression I had not: the branch-protection step's `exit 1` at `:73` ends the job before the
freshness step runs, so if `main` ever read unprotected the watchdog would be silent on both
limbs at once. Insight L242 records that the protection check is the live alarm; it does not
record that its failure gates the other one.
