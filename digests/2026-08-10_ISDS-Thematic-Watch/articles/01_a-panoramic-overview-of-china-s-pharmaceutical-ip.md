## Annotation
This article describes China's comprehensive domestic pharmaceutical IP protection framework, including patent term extension, regulatory data protection, and patent linkage systems administered by Chinese courts and agencies. While it discusses intellectual property as a covered investment concept and mentions judicial measures (Supreme People's Court tribunal decisions), it is a policy overview of a state's own IP regime rather than a dispute between an investor and a state over challenged regulatory or judicial conduct, and contains no jurisdictional/admissibility doctrine or evidence of treaty-based investor-state conflict.

## Notable line (from source)
> “From regulatory data protection and patent term extension to pre-market dispute resolution via patent linkage, targeted judicial rulings, and efficient administrative enforcement, China has constructed a full-life-cycle closed-loop pharmaceutical IP protection system that balances incentives for original innovation and public access to medicines.”
