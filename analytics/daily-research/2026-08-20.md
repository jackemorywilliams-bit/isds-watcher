## Part I — Agenda, instrument state, and the shape of this meeting

### What this record is, for a reader who has none of its history

This project watches one narrow question: investor–State arbitration in which **intellectual property**
— patents, trade secrets, clinical-trial data — is asserted as a protected investment, a **government or
court measure** is the conduct complained of, and a **live jurisdictional or admissibility doctrine** is
actually being litigated. Three rings, and an item counts only if it touches all three. The council that
watches it meets daily. It is deliberately small: a research analyst, a chairman, an integrity challenge,
and one improvement suggestion.

A reader should know two things about how this council has come to work. First, it treats **a genuinely
empty day as a real result** and says so plainly rather than manufacturing significance. Second, and less
usual, it spends a large share of its effort auditing **its own instruments** — because a research record
that cannot say what its tools can and cannot see will eventually state a tool failure as a fact about the
world. That is why a record like this one contains as much about `sha256` digests and character encodings
as about arbitration.
