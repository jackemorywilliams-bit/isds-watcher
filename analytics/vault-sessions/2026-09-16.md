## 9. External research — done this session, and reported as what it is

My mandate asks for this periodically; the last two sessions did not do it and said so. This one did,
and it was a **bounded scan of the current ecosystem, not a deep read** — I want that stated plainly
rather than dressed up.

Two things are worth carrying back:

- **The convention this vault adopted independently is now the ecosystem's convention.** Current
  writing on agent memory in Obsidian converges on two rules this project already runs: that a
  memory line rewritten *without its date* becomes unauditable, so live statements and dated records
  must be handled differently; and that staleness must be made a **query** rather than a judgement
  call. That is the snapshot-anchor convention and the "live statements change, dated records do
  not" discipline, arrived at here from failures rather than from reading. **No change proposed —
  the finding is that nothing needs changing**, which is worth knowing before a future session
  redesigns something that is already current.
- **Tooling is emerging for the thing `check_currency.py` and `check_models.py` do** — linters that
  audit an *agent instruction file* against the repository it describes, as distinct from generic
  vault checkers written for human PKM. This project built its own first. I am not recommending we
  adopt any of them; I am recording that the category now exists, so the next session evaluating our
  guards has a comparison class.
- **One genuinely applicable observation, and it is small.** The four-link cap that produces all 16
  of our graph WARNs is a sound rule for a *content* note and the wrong rule for a **map-of-content
  hub**, which exists to link many notes. Our WARNs are all index or session notes — i.e. all
  false positives by design. The cap lives in `scripts/build_graph.py`, **outside my paths**, and I
  am not proposing a change; a second `--cap` threshold for hub-class notes would silence 16 warnings
  that are currently trained to be ignored, which is its own hazard. **Systems-designer's, if it is
  anyone's.**
