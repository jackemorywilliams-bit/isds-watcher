## Part I — Agenda, instrument state, and the shape of this meeting

### What this record is, for a reader who has none of its history

This project asks one question: **at the intersection of intellectual property asserted as a covered
investment, a regulatory or judicial measure as the challenged conduct, and a live jurisdictional or
admissibility doctrine, what is investor–State dispute settlement actually doing?** The daily council is
a check-in against that question, not a research paper. Most days it returns a null, and a null recorded
plainly is the correct output of a narrow watch.

Two working threads dominate the present state. **Ring 3:** the *Hela Schwarz GmbH v. China* award is the
pivotal unread document, and the question that matters — whether the tribunal treated the China–Germany
BIT Protocol Article 9 conditions as going to **jurisdiction** or to **admissibility** — has been answered
by no source this council can reach. **Ring 1/2:** ICSID's register of decisions on *Manifest Lack of Legal
Merit* (Arbitration Rule 41(5)) states a finding per row; the values of that finding column are undecoded,
and whether that early-dismissal procedure has ever met an IP-as-investment claim is unanswered.

**Today is not a null day.** For the first time, ICSID's own case-detail page for `ARB/17/19` was read by
this council's retrieval channel, and three other never-fetched routes returned with it. What that does and
does not buy is Part II's and Part III's work, not Part I's, and I have deliberately written nothing here
that anticipates it.
