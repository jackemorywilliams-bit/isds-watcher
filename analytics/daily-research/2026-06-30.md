### B. Okuashvili v. Georgia Backsource — Swedish Supreme Court, June 15, 2026

This is the primary new doctrinal development indexed in this week's digest. Items 1 and 3 in the June 29 digest both relate to this case. Backsource reconstruction from accessible sources (UNCTAD Navigator, Jusmundi, Mondaq/Baker Botts write-ups of the Svea Court of Appeal decision, and multiple search summaries of the Supreme Court ruling):

#### B.1 Underlying case

**Claimant:** Zaza Okuashvili (dual Georgian-British national; ultimate beneficial owner of the "Omega Group").
**Respondent:** Georgia.
**Sector:** Tobacco (Omega Group Tobacco / OGT, a cigarette manufacturing company in Georgia).
**Underlying dispute:** Georgia's tax enforcement measures against OGT (unpaid taxes), leading to Georgian court-ordered seizure and compulsory sale of OGT's assets in 2018.
**Treaty invoked:** Agreement between the Government of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland and the Government of the Republic of Georgia for the Promotion and Protection of Investments (UK-Georgia BIT, 1995, in force February 15, 1995).
**SCC case number:** V 2019/058 (emergency arbitration: EA 2019/038).
**Partial Final Award on Jurisdiction and Admissibility:** August 31, 2022 — tribunal found jurisdiction. Dissent from Prof. Dr. Rolf Knieper (published on Jusmundi) argued that the contracting parties made a "conscious choice" to adopt the UK Model BIT's exclusive ICSID forum and explicitly rejected multi-forum alternatives.
Sources: [(UNCTAD Navigator, Okuashvili)](https://investmentpolicy.unctad.org/investment-dispute-settlement/cases/978/okuashvili-v-georgia); [(Jusmundi, Partial Final Award)](https://jusmundi.com/en/document/decision/en-zaza-okuashvili-v-georgia-partial-final-award-on-jurisdiction-and-admissibility-wednesday-31st-august-2022); [(Jusmundi, Dissent)](https://jusmundi.com/en/document/opinion/en-zaza-okuashvili-v-georgia-concurring-and-dissenting-opinion-of-professor-dr-rolf-knieper-wednesday-31st-august-2022).

#### B.2 MFN mechanism

The UK-Georgia BIT's Article 8 designates ICSID as the exclusive arbitral forum. Under ICSID Convention Article 25(1), a tribunal lacks jurisdiction over a claim by a national of the state that is a party to the dispute — meaning a dual Georgian-British national cannot bring a claim against Georgia at ICSID. The claimant, unable to use the exclusive designated forum, invoked Article 3 (national treatment / MFN) of the UK-Georgia BIT to import the dispute resolution clause of the Georgia-Belgium-Luxembourg Economic Union (BLEU) BIT. The Georgia-BLEU BIT allows investors a choice of arbitral fora, including SCC arbitration, and does not exclude dual nationals. The claimant sought to combine: Article 3(3) of the UK-Georgia BIT (which explicitly extends MFN treatment to "all provisions" of Articles 1–11, including Article 8 on dispute resolution) + Article 10 of the Georgia-BLEU BIT (dispute resolution with multi-forum access) → SCC jurisdiction. Sources: [(Mondaq/Baker Botts analysis, December 2024)](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/arbitration-dispute-resolution/1557888/no-consent-on-mfn-the-svea-court-of-appeal-annuls-the-award-in-zaza-okuashvili-v-georgia); [(UNCTAD Navigator)](https://investmentpolicy.unctad.org/investment-dispute-settlement/cases/978/okuashvili-v-georgia).

#### B.3 Three-instance litigation chain

1. **SCC Partial Award on Jurisdiction (August 31, 2022):** Tribunal found jurisdiction via MFN mechanism. Knieper dissented.
2. **Svea Court of Appeal (November 12, 2024):** Annulled the award for want of jurisdiction. Held: the MFN clause in the UK-Georgia BIT cannot be used to bypass the express choice of ICSID as the exclusive forum in Article 8. Georgia's counsel: Gaillard Banifatemi Shelbaya. Sources: [(GAR, "Swedish court sides with Georgia on MFN clause")](https://globalarbitrationreview.com/article/swedish-court-sides-georgia-mfn-clause); [(Baker Botts/Mondaq, December 2024)](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/arbitration-dispute-resolution/1557888/no-consent-on-mfn-the-svea-court-of-appeal-annuls-the-award-in-zaza-okuashvili-v-georgia).
3. **Swedish Supreme Court (June 15, 2026):** Reversed the Svea Court. Held: Article 3(3) of the UK-Georgia BIT explicitly extends MFN treatment to all provisions in Articles 1–11, including Article 8 on dispute settlement. The combined effect of the MFN clause and the dispute resolution clause of a third-party treaty (Georgia-BLEU BIT, Article 10) provides a valid basis for SCC jurisdiction. The SCC multi-forum access is "objectively more favourable" than the unavailable ICSID forum. Knieper dissent's "conscious choice" argument rejected. Sources: [(IAReporter, paywalled headline)](https://www.iareporter.com/articles/swedish-supreme-court-finds-that-claimant-in-okuashvili-v-georgia-could-use-mfn-detour-to-access-arbitration-under-scc-arbitration-rules/); [search-engine summaries from multiple sources at medium-high confidence].

**Calibration note:** The Swedish Supreme Court decision of June 15, 2026 is confirmed from multiple search-engine references and the IAReporter headline. The specific reasoning (Article 3(3) chain, "objectively more favourable" standard, "treaties concluded on or after" etc.) is recovered from search summaries, not from a verbatim-readable judgment text (IAReporter body is paywalled). Mark as MEDIUM-HIGH confidence on existence and outcome; MEDIUM confidence on the specific reasoning.

#### B.4 Relevance to the research question

The Okuashvili Swedish Supreme Court ruling is a Ring 3 near-miss. It advances MFN-forum-access doctrine in a Swedish-seated ISDS context, but the underlying dispute concerns Georgian tax enforcement against a tobacco company — no intellectual property, no clinical trial data, no trade secret, no IP-as-investment claim. The digest correctly scored it at 25–28 (below threshold). 

The indirect Ring 3 relevance: a future pharmaceutical investor whose applicable BIT designates an unavailable exclusive forum could attempt the same MFN-forum-access mechanism, provided the BIT contains an explicit MFN extension clause like Article 3(3) of the UK-Georgia BIT. This is a doctrinal possibility but speculative — no BIT in the China-pharmaceutical investor network has been shown to have a comparable explicit MFN extension to all BIT provisions. None of the China BITs identified in this project (China-Germany 2003, China-Switzerland 2009, China-France 2007) are Swedish-seated, and none have confirmed ICSID-exclusivity problems for European investors with dual nationality issues.

**Honest assessment:** New Ring 3 doctrine from the Swedish courts on MFN-forum-access; does not advance the pharmaceutical trade secret / clinical data thread; near-miss.
