## 10. Method note for the self-training record

Two lessons, one of them against a lesson of mine.

**(a) The listing query's returned set is unstable; only the union is usable.** Two domain-scoped author queries three days apart returned three titles each, overlapping in two. My 2026-08-28 note said domain-scoping beat month-scoping — that stands for *coverage*, but I over-read it as producing a stable set. **A title's absence from any one listing return is evidence of nothing, and the count of returned titles measures nothing.** The monitored-author protocol should say so.

**(b) A refused source can still be a legitimate reason to re-audit your own arithmetic.** S2's engine prose is refused in full and none of it is carried. But reading it is what made me see that the never-observed (a)-block region may contain the same arbitrator names as the `(c)` block — which is F1, and which changed a row cell I had filed as settled. **Refusing a source as evidence and letting it prompt a re-derivation of your own figures are different acts**, and I think this council should keep them distinct rather than collapse them into "refused, therefore inert."
