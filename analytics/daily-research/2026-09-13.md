## TASK 3 — The changed `sha256` at identical byte count

The table body was 96,161 bytes and `sha256 64eb3f52bfc496cf…` across 21 fetches in three runs on 2026-09-12. Today, fourth run, new day: **96,161 bytes, `sha256 e1ceead703fcce31…`**.

**What it licenses.** The bytes the relay received on 2026-09-13 differ from those it received on 2026-09-12, and the difference is length-preserving in aggregate. At least one byte changed and the total did not. The ordinary explanations are a fixed-width mutable field — a timestamp, a cache or session token, a rotating identifier — or a set of compensating edits, or a load-balanced pool serving variant renderings. **The relay cannot distinguish among these and I do not.** It also licenses one negative that is worth stating: yesterday's "identical across 21 fetches in three runs" describes **within-day** stability. It was never evidence of day-to-day stability and must not be carried forward as if it were.

**What it forbids.** It does not license any claim that the *table* changed: not that a row was added, amended or removed, not that any finding-column value moved, not that ICSID updated anything. The strongest available evidence points the other way — the `Spentech` window is byte-identical across the two days, so whatever changed lies **outside** `body[idx-100 : idx+300]` for the first occurrence of that literal. It forbids inferring what changed, where, or why, and it forbids reading the change as significance of any kind.

**One consequence for method, which is the reason this short task earns its place.** A whole-body `sha256` over a page with a length-preserving mutable field is a **one-way** integrity signal: equality proves the rendering was stable, inequality proves almost nothing. This project has used sha-identity as its stability anchor for this page since 2026-08-19. That anchor should move: the durable anchor is the **excerpt span itself**, which has now been returned identically across two days, two sha values, and two different find literals. Spans travel; digests do not.
