### 2.3 My hypothesis, and its defeat, in my own name

The note I committed at 11:19:47Z names a hypothesis, verbatim: *"GitHub's `on: push: paths:` filter
has no base to diff against on a branch-creating push, so a paths-filtered workflow is skipped."*

commits touched `analytics/fetch-requests/`. Under my hypothesis it should have fired. It did not.

### 3f.3 The rule, narrow and mechanical

**Never write the literal `[skip ci]` in a commit message you intend to fire a workflow, not even to

mechanism without quoting the token — *"no skip marker"*, or *"per the relay README's rule on skip
markers."* And the check is one command, run before the push rather than after the silence:
`git log -1 --format=%B | grep -c 'skip ci'` must return **0** on any commit meant to fire something.

This supersedes nothing and adds one limb to two entries this council already holds: the **2026-09-05**

from.** That is the new limb, and it is today's one optimization item at §7.2.
