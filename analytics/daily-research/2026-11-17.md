## 3. The day's substantive finding — the dead-man's switch was owed twice, and sent nothing

**Plain statement of the finding for a reader with no prior context.** This project runs an automated
watchdog whose single job is to email Emory if the daily council meeting stops happening. It is a
dead-man's switch: silence from the council is supposed to produce noise in Emory's inbox. The council
**did** stop happening — no sitting convened on 2026-11-15 or 2026-11-16, and the Monday roundtable did
not convene either. **The watchdog ran on schedule on both of those days, examined the record, declared
the meeting "fresh", and sent nothing.** Emory was never told. The gap was discovered only because
today's scheduled session happened to convene on the third day and read the directory.

### 3.1 The mechanism was already known, and I name the prior art before my own evidence

**This is not a new diagnosis and I do not present it as one.** `analytics/insights.jsonl:242`
(2026-10-06) already establishes it, and in stronger terms than I would have reached unaided:

> `heartbeat.yml:76`'s daily-meeting staleness watchdog — the watchdog on the daily council itself —
> keys on `os.path.getmtime`, and git stores no mtimes, so `actions/checkout` resets every file on every
> run and `age_h` is approximately zero ALWAYS. Measured in a fresh clone: 2 distinct mtimes across 108
> records; a 105-day-old record reading as seconds old; the step's own computation returns
> `age_h = 0.029`, `stale = False`. **IT CANNOT FIRE AT ANY STALENESS.**

That entry is **42 days old**. The defect has been carried to Emory as escalation 3 on every sitting
since, and `getmtime` appears in **11** of this council's daily records. **I add no mechanism and I
claim no diagnosis.**

### 3.2 What is new: until this week the dead limb had never once been owed

The 2026-10-06 entry's own bounds clause is the reason the defect stayed theoretical, and it is worth
quoting because it names exactly the case that has now arrived:

> **A DEAD ALARM IS NOT EVIDENCE OF A DEAD PIPELINE, AND NOT EVIDENCE OF A HEALTHY ONE.** The daily
> council has in fact run every day (97 delivery markers, newest 2026-10-05) …

That was true when it was written and it is why nobody could show a cost. **The council had never missed
two consecutive days, so the switch had never been owed an alert, so "it cannot fire" was a prediction

The schedule drifts. The cron is `0 22 * * *` — 22:00 UTC — and the three runs fired at **01:29Z**,

original one.
