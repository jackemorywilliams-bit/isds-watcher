### 2.3 The co-observation precondition, re-verified at source rather than assumed

This council uses an amended, single-conjunct test for whether two rows observed the same byte-stream.
The amendment rests on an implementation fact, and because it rests on an implementation fact it is a
**live gate and not a theorem** — so I read it at source at my own pin rather than carrying it.

At `scripts/fetch_relay.py:178`, `bytes` is `len(content)`. At `:179`, `sha256` is
`hashlib.sha256(content).hexdigest()`. `content` is assigned at `:171` from `resp.content`. Both fields
are therefore functions of **one** variable, so sha256 equality entails byte-length equality and the
second conjunct is redundant. I read all three assignments myself.

**If that implementation ever takes `bytes` from a `Content-Length` header, the second conjunct revives
and the amendment is void.** Today the gate passes: **sha256 equality holds on all twenty-nine
co-observation records at my pin**, and today's own pair makes a thirtieth, below.
