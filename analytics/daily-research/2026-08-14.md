### The one optimization item (Rule 0)

**Today's item is the one the chairman seat keeps paying for and has not fixed: the relay's
lexicographic request selection is not merely a trap, it is an *unmonitored* trap.**
`newest_request()` sorts by filename despite its name (`scripts/fetch_relay.py:233-235`) and `run()`
writes to `out_dir / request_path.name` with no existence check, so a mis-sorted second batch silently
re-runs and overwrites a completed one. The fix is on the operator's PENDING list and is not applied
here. But the *observation* worth adding is that the failure is **invisible in the artifact**: an
overwritten reduction looks exactly like a fresh one, and the 2026-08-13 seat only caught it because
it happened to notice a `fetched_at` it did not expect. The cheapest partial mitigation available
inside the existing pending patch is not a new feature but one line of it — **refuse to overwrite an
existing result file** — because that converts a silent corruption into a loud failure. That is the
whole item; nothing was built and nothing was applied. **Do NOT accept an mtime-based fix:
`actions/checkout` destroys mtimes.**
