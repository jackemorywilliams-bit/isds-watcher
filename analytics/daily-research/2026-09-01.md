## Part I — Agenda, instrument state, and the shape of this meeting

### What this record is, for a reader who has none of its history

This project watches for a narrow intersection: **intellectual property — including trade secrets and
clinical-trial data — asserted as a covered investment**, against **a regulatory or judicial measure** as the
disputed conduct, with **a live, litigated jurisdictional or admissibility doctrine** such as abuse of right or
treaty-shopping. The intersection is deliberately narrow, and a genuinely quiet day is an honest result rather
than a failure.

For seven consecutive sessions this council's substantive work has run on one docket — **ICSID Case No.
ARB/12/1, *Tethyan Copper Company Pty Limited v. Islamic Republic of Pakistan*** — and the reason is not that
the docket answers the research question. It does not; it is a mineral-exploration dispute with no
intellectual-property nexus. It is the one place where this council's own instrument can read primary material
that no secondary source carries, and so it is where the instrument gets calibrated. **That distinction is
restated at the top of every record because it is the single most available way for this project to mislead
its own operator**, and today, which is a good day for the instrument, is exactly the day it most needs saying.

The instrument needs one sentence for a new reader. **This session has no internet access of its own.** What it
has is a *relay*: it writes a small JSON file naming a handful of URLs and a literal string to look for in
each, pushes that file to GitHub, and a GitHub Actions runner — which does have network access — fetches each
page and commits back a *reduction*: the HTTP status, the byte length, a SHA-256 digest of the page, and a
single 400-character excerpt centred on the first place the literal occurs. **The document itself never travels
and is never stored.** Every factual question this council can answer from the primary docket is answered
through a 400-character window, and most of the machinery below exists to keep the council honest about what a
400-character window can and cannot establish.

### The member return-path protocol

Every member of this council is a **real subagent**, spawned with the repository's own committed definitions in
`.claude/agents/`. No seat's words are written by me, and no seat is performed. Each member files its return as
its final message and that text enters this record **verbatim** — I rule on it, I do not edit it. Where a
seat's actual model differs from the operator's directive, the seat reports the discrepancy unasked and it is
recorded as a procedural caveat rather than silently absorbed. **If a spawn fails, the seat is recorded as a
PROCEDURAL FAILURE for the day and is not role-played.** That is the operator's cardinal rule and the one
failure this project treats as unrecoverable.
