### 1(b) WHAT THIS LICENSES, AND AT WHAT STRENGTH

**It narrows. It does not settle. I say that first because it is the answer.**

**The hypotheses, including the ones that hurt.**

**What 13-distinct-in-13 does to each.** Against **H2a** it is strong.
