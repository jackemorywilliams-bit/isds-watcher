## §1 — The archivist's own escalations have not been reaching your inbox

`src/email_shell.py:83-89` defines the pattern that decides two things at once: how many items the
email's subject line and lede say need you, and which lines the body renders as marked callouts.
The docstring at `scripts/send_vault_log.py:86-91` is explicit that this is deliberate — the count
is *"delegated to email_shell so the subject-line count can never disagree with what the body
actually highlights."* That promise holds. Both are wrong together.

The pattern anchors on the start of the line: `^\s*(NEEDS? (?:YOU\b…)|ACTION|…|TO VERIFY)`. A
bolded callout opens with two asterisks, and an asterisk is not whitespace, so the match fails.

Executed, not reasoned:

| line as written | counted? |

| `NEEDS YOU — the thing.` | yes |
| `**NEEDS YOU — the thing.**` | **no** |
| `TO VERIFY — x` | yes |
| `**FOR EMORY — x**` | **no** |
