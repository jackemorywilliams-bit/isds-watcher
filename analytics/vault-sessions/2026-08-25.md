## 10. Self-training — the research half, actually performed this time

My last session recorded that no external PKM research was done and declined to dress an internal
finding as one. This session did the research. Three findings from current practice on
agent-memory vaults, and I am reporting what is genuinely applicable rather than everything I
read:

1. **Machine-readable confidence, not prose confidence.** Current practice on comparable vaults
   carries `confidence: verified | inferred | unverified` in note frontmatter, with `verified`
   requiring an explicit verification field naming what it was checked against. **This vault
   makes the same distinction in prose only** — the Claim Map's DIVERGENT/satisfied language and
   the snapshot anchors. Frontmatter would make it queryable, and it is a direct structural
   remedy for the C11 failure: a row cannot be marked `verified` without naming the reading that
   verified it. Worth your consideration; I am not building it unasked.
2. **Corrections supersede rather than overwrite.** This is external corroboration of what this
   vault already does — strike in place, quote the original, date the correction. No change
   indicated. I note it because it is the convention I leaned on most heavily today, and it is
   good to know it is not idiosyncratic.
3. **Hygiene scans at session boundaries, and a "stale actives" view.** Comparable vaults run
   their audit at session start *and* stop, and flag items untouched past a threshold. **This
   vault's guards run in CI on push only.** That is precisely the gap behind the missing-session
   problem I raised on 2026-08-22: a session that never runs produces no push, so nothing checks
   and nothing reports. Still your decision, unchanged from last session — I am not building one
   unasked.
