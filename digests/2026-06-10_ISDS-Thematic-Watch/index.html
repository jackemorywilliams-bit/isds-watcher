1. Hydro S.r.l. and others v. Republic of Albania (I), ICSID Case No. ARB/15/28

Italaw  ·  26 May 2026  ·  Watch · 28

This order concerns a provisional measures application and revision proceeding in a dispute between Hydro S.r.l. and Albania, involving criminal and tax proceedings allegedly retaliatory to the arbitration, and a subsequent challenge to a Tirana court judgment on revision grounds. The case involves a domestic criminal judgment and allegations of procedural impropriety, but fails to trigger the three-ring theme because there is no intellectual property at stake, the judicial measure is tangential to the core investment dispute (which concerns water concessions or similar assets, not IP), and the jurisdictional questions (Article 51 revision grounds, pre-award vs. post-award facts) do not engage abuse-of-right, treaty-shopping, or standing doctrines relevant to IP-as-investment.

“The Tribunal held that the Tirana Judgment, being a post-award event, could not constitute a pre-award 'fact' capable of supporting revision under Article 51 of the ICSID Convention.”
Source: Italaw: https://www.italaw.com/cases/3958

2. Telefónica, S.A. v. Republic of Colombia, ICSID Case No. ARB/18/3

Italaw  ·  02 Jun 2026  ·  Watch · 28

A telecommunications investor challenged Colombia's Constitutional Court judgment and legislative measures that altered asset-reversion rules for its concession, alleging breach of fair-and-equitable treatment and frustration of legitimate expectations. While the dispute involves a judicial measure (the Constitutional Court's Sentencia C-555), it concerns telecommunications concession assets and contractual reversion obligations, not intellectual property, and raises no IP-as-investment angle, abuse-of-right, treaty-shopping, or shell-subsidiary doctrine; the case thus falls below the thematic intersection and scores LOW.

“It affirmed its jurisdiction to assess whether state measures, including judicial acts, complied with the treaty, irrespective of a denial of justice claim.”
Source: Italaw: https://www.italaw.com/cases/12153

Tags: judicial_measure · constitutional_court · res_judicata · legitimate_expectations · fair_and_equitable_treatment
