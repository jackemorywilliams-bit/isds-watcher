## How it was landed, and why not by merging #341

**PR #341 was not merged, and merging it would have breached the contract it was caught by.** Its
eight paths include `STATE_OF_THE_ANSWER.md`, so the pre-merge scope test forbids a self-merge —
the instruction is explicit that if any path falls outside the record set, do not merge. Instead I
**cherry-picked `e2d60664` alone** onto this session's branch as **`b9224999`**, excluding the
bot's commit entirely. All six of its paths — `HANDOFF.md`, four notes under `agents/`, and
`analytics/vault-sessions/2026-11-19.md` — are inside the authorised set, and the anchor churn is
left for `reanchor.py` to redo on its own terms. **PR #341 is therefore superseded at content
level rather than merged.**

**FOR EMORY — PR #341 can be closed.** Its entire payload is on `main` as of this session. I have
left it open rather than close it myself, because closing another sitting's pull request is not a
record-path action and the decision is yours.

**The delivery mechanism is why the silence was total.** `.github/workflows/vault-log.yml` runs
daily at 16:00 UTC and emails any record in `analytics/vault-sessions/` that has no marker in
`.sent/`. **It reads `main`.** An unlanded record is therefore never emailed and raises no alarm —
markers run `2026-10-04` through `2026-11-07` and then stop. Landing the file restores it to the
emailer's view, which is why you receive both records today.

**It could not have caught itself, and that is structural.** The 2026-11-19 sitting swept all
**312** remote branch tips and reported its orphan check clean — correctly, because its own branch
did not yet exist when the sweep ran. **The only possible detector is the next sitting.** Adopted
as **rule 15**, with **step 5** added to the merge procedure: confirm ancestry after merging, land
the record before polishing it, and open the orphan check with this seat's own branches rather
than finding one stranded sitting as a single line among 119 non-ancestors.
