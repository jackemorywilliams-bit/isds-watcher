> **N** distinct `(path, blob)` pairs under `<path>`, over `git rev-list <TIP>` × `git ls-tree -r`, at TIP `<sha>`, **in a clone whose `.git/shallow` was absent — verified by `git rev-parse --is-shallow-repository` returning `false` at the time of the count** — counted `<date>`, over `<named ref set>`.
