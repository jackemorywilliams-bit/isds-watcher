### 2A. Suez v. Argentina (1) — Full Backsource

**Case identity confirmed:** *Suez, Sociedad General de Aguas de Barcelona S.A., and InterAguas Servicios Integrales del Agua S.A. v. The Argentine Republic*, ICSID Case No. ARB/03/17 (registered 2003; Decision on Jurisdiction May 16, 2006; Award December 4, 2015; Annulment denied December 14, 2018). [(UNCTAD Navigator)](https://investmentpolicy.unctad.org/investment-dispute-settlement/cases/120/suez-and-interagua-v-argentina); [(italaw)](https://www.italaw.com/cases/1048); [(Jusmundi, Annulment)](https://jusmundi.com/en/document/decision/en-suez-sociedad-general-de-aguas-de-barcelona-s-a-and-interagua-servicios-integrales-de-agua-s-a-v-argentine-republic-decision-on-argentinas-application-for-annulment-friday-14th-december-2018)

**Investor structure:** Suez (incorporated in France) and AGBAR / InterAguas (both incorporated in Spain) as major shareholders in Aguas Provinciales de Santa Fe S.A. (Argentine operating company), which held a 30-year water distribution and wastewater concession in the Province of Santa Fe, Argentina. Treaty bases: France-Argentina BIT (Suez); Spain-Argentina BIT (AGBAR, InterAguas).

**Core MFN holding (Decision on Jurisdiction, May 16, 2006):** Tribunal (President: Prof. Jeswald Salacuse; Prof. Gabrielle Kaufmann-Kohler; Prof. Pedro Nikken) held that AGBAR and InterAguas were entitled, by operation of the MFN clause in the Argentina-Spain BIT, to invoke the Argentina-France BIT's more favorable dispute settlement provision. The Spain-Argentina BIT, Article X, required investors to litigate in Argentine courts for 18 months before commencing ICSID arbitration. The France-Argentina BIT imposed no equivalent pre-arbitration local court requirement. The MFN clause allowed AGBAR and InterAguas to bypass the 18-month local litigation requirement entirely and proceed directly to ICSID. [(Trans-Lex.org)](https://www.trans-lex.org/240110/_/icisd-decision-suez-v-argentina-icsid-arb-03-17-19/); [(search engine summary of tribunal documents at icsidfiles.worldbank.org)](http://icsidfiles.worldbank.org/icsid/icsidblobs/OnlineAwards/C18/DC689_En.pdf)

**Subsequent procedural history:** Merits hearing → Award (December 4, 2015) finding Argentina breached FET and ordering compensation → Annulment application by Argentina → ICSID Annulment Committee denied annulment (December 14, 2018). [(Jusmundi, annulment decision)](https://jusmundi.com/en/document/decision/en-suez-sociedad-general-de-aguas-de-barcelona-s-a-and-interagua-servicios-integrales-de-agua-s-a-v-argentine-republic-decision-on-argentinas-application-for-annulment-friday-14th-december-2018); academic analysis in AJIL: [(Cambridge Core, Akinkugbe)](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-journal-of-international-law/article/zaza-okuashvili-v-georgia-case-v-2019058/452908C87583F9F2CB54BAF0AD70CBF7)

**Ring relevance assessment:**
- **No IP nexus.** Water utility sector; Argentine tariff adjustments; no trade secrets, no pharmaceutical data, no intellectual property as covered investment. This is not a three-ring case.
- **Ring 3 near-miss.** The case is the canonical example of the "Maffezini line" — MFN bypass of a pre-arbitration exhaustion/local remedies condition. The bypass changes the procedural precondition only; the forum (ICSID) remains the same. This is doctrinally distinct from the Okuashvili mechanism (MFN import of an entirely different forum: ICSID → SCC).
- **This item does not advance the pharmaceutical/trade-secret research question.** Confirmed as a near-miss on all three rings.

**Why IAReporter published this now (analytical observation):** The "Looking Back" retrospective appeared July 6, 2026 — ten days after the Swedish Supreme Court's June 26, 2026 ruling in Okuashvili reinstating the MFN-forum-access doctrine. IAReporter's editorial timing is consistent with contextualizing Suez (1) as historical background for the resurgent MFN-clause debate: the Maffezini/Suez-type bypass of pre-arbitration conditions (same forum; procedural requirement removed) is the antecedent to Okuashvili's extension to forum import (different forum; structural consent question). The article body is paywalled and this is an editorial inference from timing only — not a finding.

### 2B. Analytical Corollary: Chinese BIT MFN Exclusion Covers Suez-Type Pre-Condition Bypass (Inferential)

The Suez (1) backsource raises an analytical corollary to the July 2 finding on Chinese BIT MFN exclusion.

The Suez-type MFN bypass (bypassing a pre-arbitration condition via MFN, while retaining the same forum) is a distinct mechanism from Okuashvili-type (importing an entirely different forum). A European pharmaceutical investor challenging a Chinese NMPA pharmaceutical data exclusivity decision under the China-Germany BIT (2003) might attempt a Suez-type bypass: invoke MFN from the China-Switzerland BIT (or another Chinese BIT) to argue that the Hela Schwarz concurrent-proceedings/court-withdrawal requirement can be bypassed because the invoked treaty imposes no equivalent pre-arbitration condition. Under Suez (1) logic — in the right treaty architecture — this might succeed.

The existing record (July 2 insight, MEDIUM confidence) establishes that Chinese BITs explicitly exclude MFN extension to "procedural rights to dispute settlement." The SMU Law Review source [(SMU Law Review, "BITs, MFN Treatment and the PRC")](https://scholar.smu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1305&context=til) confirms that "recent Chinese BITs have eliminated potential controversies over the scope of the MFN clause by making clear that the MFN treatment does not cover the procedural rights to dispute settlement." This language is broad: "procedural rights to dispute settlement" encompasses both forum choice (Okuashvili-type) and pre-arbitration procedural conditions (Suez-type). Accordingly, the same MFN exclusion that closes mechanism 4 (forum bypass) also closes the Suez-type pre-condition bypass pathway.

**Analytical corollary:** the Ring 3 taxonomy's mechanism 4 label ("MFN-scope-based forum bypass") should be read broadly as foreclosing all MFN-based procedural ISDS bypass — whether forum import (Okuashvili) or pre-condition bypass (Suez). Both pathways are architecturally closed for European pharmaceutical investors under the China-Germany (2003), China-Switzerland (2009), and China-France (2007) BITs.

**Confidence: MEDIUM for the underlying MFN exclusion; LOW for the specific Suez/Hela Schwarz scenario.** This is an analytical inference combining two established findings. No case has litigated this specific Suez-type bypass under Chinese BITs; no practitioner source makes the connection explicitly. The security officer will flag; this is a precision refinement of the existing finding, not a new sourced insight.
