### What this record is, for a reader who has none of its history

This project watches for a narrow intersection: **intellectual property — including trade secrets and
clinical-trial data — asserted as a covered investment**, against **a regulatory or judicial measure** as the
disputed conduct, with **a live, litigated jurisdictional or admissibility doctrine** such as abuse of right or
treaty-shopping. The intersection is deliberately narrow, and a genuinely quiet day is an honest result rather
than a failure.

For seven consecutive sessions this council's substantive work has run on one docket — **ICSID Case No.
ARB/12/1, *Tethyan Copper Company Pty Limited v. Islamic Republic of Pakistan*** — and the reason is not that
the docket answers the research question. It does not; it is a mineral-exploration dispute with no
intellectual-property nexus. It is the one place where this council's own instrument can read primary material

restated at the top of every record because it is the single most available way for this project to mislead

The instrument needs one sentence for a new reader. **This session has no internet access of its own.** What it
