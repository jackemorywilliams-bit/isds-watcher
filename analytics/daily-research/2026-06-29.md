Source: [(Kluwer Arbitration Blog)](https://legalblogs.wolterskluwer.com/arbitration-blog/inaugural-washington-arbitration-week-discussions-on-isds-reform-a-light-at-the-end-of-the-tunnel/).

#### A.3 What the pre-session record now establishes

Integrating these new sources with the prior week's research, the pre-session picture is as follows:

- **What is expected to be adopted (Protocol track):** A/CN.9/1246 — the Procedural Protocol containing ISDS-specific provisions on evidence, bifurcation, interim measures, manifest lack of legal merit, security for costs, TPF disclosure, counterclaims, and cost allocation. This package was "bundled" at the 53rd session to sidestep selective-adoption debates; the 54th session completed drafting; the ICLRC "almost finalised" note confirms it is substantively ready for Commission adoption. This is the clearest deliverable. Source: [(IISD ITN, April 2026)](https://www.iisd.org/itn/2026/04/21/uncitral-working-group-iii-advances-isds-reform-but-progress-remains-slow/).

- **What is expected to be considered (MIC / MIIR tracks):** The 54th session (March 2026, Vienna) produced draft statutes for a first-tier standing mechanism and an appellate standing mechanism. These are more contested; the July 2026 Commission session is expected to endorse them as draft instruments for further negotiation, not adopt final statutes. Source: [(UNCITRAL, MIC page)](https://uncitral.un.org/en/multilateralpermanentinvestmentcourt).

- **Advisory Centre:** A/CN.9/1265, 1266, and 1268 (the draft Commission decision on Article 9 of the Statute) are on the agenda. The Kluwer post implies the Advisory Centre statute has already been adopted in principle; the July 2026 session likely addresses the remaining operational question (Article 9 decision). Source: [(Kluwer Arbitration Blog)](https://legalblogs.wolterskluwer.com/arbitration-blog/inaugural-washington-arbitration-week-discussions-on-isds-reform-a-light-at-the-end-of-the-tunnel/).

- **What remains absent:** The denial-of-benefits provision (Draft Provision 9, WP.253) is confirmed not in A/CN.9/1246 and not on the July 2026 agenda. The natural-person nationality-planning gap from Jason Yu Song v. China is not addressed. Ring 3 doctrine architecture is therefore unchanged heading into today's session. Source: [(UNCITRAL, A/CN.9/1246 advance copy)](https://uncitral.un.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/a_cn9_1246_advance_copy.pdf).

- **Architecture:** The opt-in architecture is confirmed. No state that opposes any reform element is bound, whether through the UNCITRAL Arbitration Rules or through the MIIR. Source: [(EJIL:Talk, "What to Expect When You're Expecting")](https://www.ejiltalk.org/uncitral-and-isds-reform-what-to-expect-when-youre-expecting/).

#### A.4 African ISDS Workshop (Rabat, June 3–5, 2026)

A preparatory regional workshop on implementation of ISDS reform for African states was held in Rabat, Morocco, June 3–5, 2026, in advance of the Commission session.
