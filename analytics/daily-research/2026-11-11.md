### 6.5 Where this leaves the research question — in plain language, assuming nothing

This section is written for a reader who has never seen this project. `COUNCIL.md` requires it,

**What we are looking for.** The project watches investor–State arbitration — cases where a

combination. We want a live case in which all three of these are true at once: the company's
asserted investment is **intellectual property** (a patent, a trademark, a trade secret, or

the case at all — abuse of process, treaty-shopping, or whether the dispute was already foreseeable
when the investor restructured. Three decided cases are the model: *Philip Morris v Australia*,
*Eli Lilly v Canada*, and *Bridgestone v Panama*.

answer rather than a disappointing one.

that as a signal. **It is not one.** Nearly every arbitration's first procedural order covers
confidentiality as routine housekeeping. Reading it as evidence of a trade-secret dispute would be
inventing a finding, and both the analyst and I declined to do it.
