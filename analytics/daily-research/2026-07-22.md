The 9(a) disposition upgrade from UNCONFIRMED to MEDIUM-HIGH is the session's genuine advance.

**On the Okuashvili four-factor test:**

The four-factor framework (textual scope, ejusdem generis, objective holistic comparison, unambiguous consent construction) enriches the project's background Ring 3 doctrine without advancing the pharmaceutical China BIT question, since Chinese BITs architecturally exclude MFN extension to ISDS procedural rights (July 2, 2026 confirmed finding). The framework's value is as a benchmark test — if a non-China BIT scenario arises where MFN-forum-access is contested, the SC's test is now documented in the record.
