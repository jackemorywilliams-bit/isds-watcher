UNCITRAL 59th Session — document architecture verified, disposition unresolved

No authoritative post-session source confirming the Commission's disposition of the draft was retrievable this session; the coverage lag flagged in the daily notes is still running, so the no-adoption line holds. What was verified against UNCITRAL's own Working Group III docket is the primary-document architecture the project had carried only from pre-session snippets. A/CN.9/1246 is the draft supplementary provisions on the conduct of proceedings to resolve international investment disputes (advance copy); the advance-copy date of 15 April 2026 was not confirmed against a fetched PDF and should be treated as an unverified snippet-level date. The document is a draft placed before the Commission, not an adopted instrument, and the standalone-instrument framing remains at medium confidence pending the post-session report.

The Advisory Centre work is a separate Commission track. The document identifiers associated with it (reported as A/CN.9/1265, 1266, and 1268, and a Commission-level A/CN.9/1270 dated 24 June 2026) could not be body-verified this session — the PDFs were not fetched — and should be treated as unverified until a named retrieved source confirms each number and description. A UNCITRAL notice described as a final call for Governments to express interest in hosting the Advisory Centre appears to exist at snippet level, but its date relative to the session is uncertain; the notice does not by itself establish host status at session close, and no host should be recorded as selected without a named source.

A methodological ceiling bears noting: the supplementary provisions govern conduct of proceedings, not jurisdiction or admissibility gatekeeping, so their direct bearing on Ring 3 is limited and should not be overstated. Recheck IISD ITN and EJIL:Talk in mid-to-late July for the actual disposition of A/CN.9/1246, the MIC/MIIR track, and the host outcome.
