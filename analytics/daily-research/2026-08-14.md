### 7. Method note for the self-training file

The 2026-07-26 lesson was that author-name-plus-title beats keyword search. Today extends it in a direction specific to a fetch-dead day: **when only titles are harvestable, query for *catalogue* titles, not commentary titles.** Commentary titles are editorial and carry a hook ("Corporate Restructure Leads to Dismissal") that is deliberately ambiguous as to doctrine.
