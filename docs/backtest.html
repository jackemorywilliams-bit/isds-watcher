Scorer evaluation

Historical backtest

A small exploratory backtest on known cases — not a comprehensive validation. It scores familiar investor–State cases with the same deterministic keyword scorer the pipeline uses, at the same digest threshold (40), and reports the results in three separate buckets so the cases that trained the fingerprint never inflate the headline.

Why three buckets

An earlier version of this page folded every case into one blended confusion matrix. That flattered the scorer: the development seed awards are the very cases whose vocabulary defines the fingerprint, so recovering them proves only that the system recognises what generated it — circular, not predictive. To keep the headline honest, the cases are split into three clearly-labelled buckets, each reported on its own:

  • Seed recovery — the three development seed awards, scored in-sample. A sanity check that the fingerprint recognises its own source material. Its numbers never enter any headline.
  • Out-of-sample holdout — four held-out positives and clear off-theme negatives, none of them used in development. This is the headline generalisation metric: precision, recall, accuracy and F1 are computed on this bucket alone.
  • Live prospective — the live weekly digest archive, the only test that scores cases the system has never seen before the outcome is known. Reported as an honest status, not as numbers from this corpus.

Every case’s text comes from material already in the repository — the seeds from their verbatim seed-tagged phrases in fingerprint.yaml, the holdout items verbatim from holdout_set.json. Nothing here is fabricated, and any case whose text could not be resolved in-repo was excluded rather than invented.

Where it misses, and why

False negatives

  • Apotex Inc. v. United States of America (UNCT/10/2, 2013) (score 8). Apotex turns on a negative jurisdictional finding — the tribunal held the claimant was not an investor and the FDA filings were not a covered investment. The deterministic keyword scorer keys on doctrinal vocabulary asserting a theme; a holding that the theme is ABSENT under-detects, since 'not a covered investment' reads as a near-miss on the IP-as-investment ring rather than a positive hit.

Bucket 3 — live prospective

Live prospective

Status: Zero direct thematic matches to date.

The live weekly digest archive is the true prospective test — the only one that scores items the system has never seen, in real time, before the outcome is known. To date it has produced no direct thematic hit: across the published weeks not a single live listing has cleared the digest threshold as an on-theme match. What it has shown is the discipline you would want from a watcher running quiet — clean negative filtering of off-theme noise (ICSID procedural notices, ITN headlines, OECD guidance) and watch-list triage of near-misses held below threshold, not a live direct hit. Treat the holdout numbers above as the generalisation estimate and this archive as the honest, still-open prospective record.

See the digest archive for the live record, and the methodology for how the scorer and its threshold are defined.
