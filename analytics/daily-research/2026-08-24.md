### The premature-email defect has a remedy this session, and it is one that costs nothing

Yesterday's `N1` is the sharpest operational item the council holds: `daily-update.yml` fires on any
**unskipped** push touching `analytics/daily-research/**`, on any branch, so the chairman's Part I commit
emailed the operator a one-Part "daily meeting record" and then wrote the per-day de-duplication marker that
would have suppressed the finished one. The remedy applied yesterday was to delete the marker before merge —
the second time in this project's history — and the integrity officer's census found the marker had preceded
the finished record on **at least eight days, caught once.**

**There is a cheaper remedy and it was in the workflow's own comments the whole time.** `daily-update.yml`
lines 8–11 state that the routine *"commits with `[skip ci]`, which GitHub honors to SKIP workflows"*, and
that the push trigger *"will NOT fire on the routine's own commit."* That is the designed behaviour; the
sessions that tripped `N1` simply did not carry the token. **Every incremental Part commit in this session
carries `[skip ci]`.** No premature email is sent, no premature marker is written, and the complete record is
delivered by the merge push itself. This changes no file outside the record paths — it is a commit-message
discipline, not a build. **It does not close `N1`**, because it depends on every future session remembering,
and the durable fix is one condition in `scripts/send_daily_update.py`, outside this council's merge scope.

**The time-box from yesterday's item still binds:** `date_str` is `datetime.now(UTC)`, so the merge must
complete **today, UTC**, or the record is never emailed at all.
