### 1.5 THE SERVIER/POLAND FORUM ROW IS NOT FIRED, AND THAT IS A RULING WITH A DEFECT BEHIND IT

This row was queued by the chairman on 2026-10-01 to *"the cadenced batch on or about 2026-10-13"* —

being fired late either. **It cannot be fired at all, because the ground on which it was queued is
false, and I established that by execution at session open rather than inheriting it.**

The queuing ruling is recorded in the thread (gap slug
`servier-poland-2012-forum-pca-or-ad-hoc-uncitral`) in these words: *"analytics/locked_set/
VALIDATION_RECORD.md row 1-03 already identifies pca-cpa.org as a locator for this caption — so a PCA
case-register row is relay-reachable, is NOT a discovery hunt under the relay's own header."*

**Row 1-03 is a different matter.** `VALIDATION_RECORD.md:290` reads
`| 1-03 | Merck & Co., Inc. v. Republic of Ecuador … | PCA Case No. 2012-10 (US–Ecuador BIT,
UNCITRAL) | pca-cpa.org — case page for this caption |`. The Servier row is **1-02**, at `:289`, and
its candidate locator is `italaw.com — case page for this caption (numeric case id **not verified**)`.
So the pca-cpa.org locator belongs to Merck v. Ecuador, not to Servier, and the Servier row's own
locator is on a host under this council's standing fetch prohibition.

every `.md`, `.json` and `.jsonl` in the repository returns **no pca-cpa.org locator tied to the

repeating it. (ii) PCA case pages are addressed as `/en/cases/<numeric id>/`; this project holds four
