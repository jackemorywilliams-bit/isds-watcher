### 1(b) WHAT THIS LICENSES, AND AT WHAT STRENGTH

**It narrows. It does not settle. I say that first because it is the answer.**

**The hypotheses, including the ones that hurt.**

- **H1 — uncontrolled free text**: a per-case editorial summary.
- **H2a — a controlled vocabulary of small or moderate size**, comparable in scale to `Economic Sector`.
- **H2b — a controlled vocabulary of large size**, hundreds of terms.
- **H3 — semi-controlled by accretion**: a maintained list to which a new term is added whenever no existing term fits. At n=13 this is operationally indistinguishable from H1, and it is the hypothesis nobody has put on the table.

**What 13-distinct-in-13 does to each.** Against **H2a** it is strong. Under uniform draws from a vocabulary of size K, P(all 13 distinct) is 0.0000 at K=11, 0.0059 at K=20, 0.047 at K=30, 0.18 at K=50. Against **H2b** it does nothing at all: K=200 gives 0.67, K=500 gives 0.85. Between **H1, H2b and H3** it discriminates **not at all**.
