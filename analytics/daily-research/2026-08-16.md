### The one optimization item (Rule 0)

**Half of everything this instrument has ever returned is truncated at both ends by construction, and
the council has now been bitten by the right-hand end twice in two days. The fix is not vigilance; it
is a reading rule that follows from the artefact's own geometry, and it costs nothing.** Derived over
the whole corpus — `analytics/fetch-results/*.json`, comparing each record's `excerpt` length against
its manifest's own `excerpt_chars` — **91 excerpts carry text, and 46 of them (50.5%) sit exactly at
the cap.** An excerpt at the cap is a fixed-width slice of a whitespace-collapsed string: it begins
mid-token and ends mid-token, and *which* token it ends in is an accident of where the `find` landed,
not a fact about the source. So **the last whitespace-delimited token of any capped excerpt is
untrustworthy by construction, always, without a seat having to notice anything.**

Today establishes the mechanism by character equality rather than by inference, which is why I think it
is worth adopting rather than merely repeating. The 08-15 and 08-16 windows on the *Einarsson* docket
overlap by **177 characters** (derived by longest suffix-prefix equality over `2026-08-15-daily.json`
`records[2].excerpt` and `2026-08-16-daily.json` `records[2].excerpt`). The overlap **ends** with
`Robert Hobbs R`; today's new text **begins** `WS-03 - Witness Statement of Carl Makrides`. The
trailing `R` the record has been carrying since yesterday was the first character of `RWS-03`. That is
the same mechanism that turned `Expert Report of “Chip” Gordon` into `Expert Report of “Chip” Gordon
C. Gill` on 08-15, and it is now observed twice on one page in two days.

**Three honest limits, stated because the rule is both weaker and narrower than it sounds.** First,
**it is a rule about the artefact, not about any source** — it says which characters we may not trust,
and nothing whatever about ICSID, the PCA or any docket. Second, **the rule governs BOTH edges, and
cappedness governs only the right one.** With a `find`, `scripts/fetch_relay.py:134` opens the window
at `idx - 100`, so any excerpt whose first match sat past character 100 begins mid-token regardless of
length — today's own windows open `LP (Public Version)` and `s Written submission`. Third, **a
below-cap excerpt IS trustworthy at its right edge, and this is derived rather than left open**:
`excerpt_of` has only two return paths, both slices of a `.strip()`ed body, so a non-empty excerpt
shorter than the cap ends where the reduced body ends. The 46/91 figure is the population where the
**right-edge** cut is guaranteed; the left-edge cut has a different and larger population, which I have
not derived. **And the tokenisation itself is instrument-produced**: per Part III E7, every stripped tag
becomes a space, so "whitespace-delimited" is the relay's word division and not the page's. Nothing was
built and no code change is proposed; `scripts/` is outside this session's scope and no approval exists.
