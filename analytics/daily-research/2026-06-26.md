### A. Thread T04 — Germany-China BIT (2003) and France-China BIT (2007): Investment Definitions Verified

#### A.1 China-Germany BIT (2003) — "trade and business secrets" plus "know-how"

Multiple authoritative treaty databases consistently report the following provision. Article 1 of the China-Germany BIT (2003) defines "investment" to include intellectual property rights encompassing: *"copyrights, patents and industrial designs, trade-marks, trade-names, technical processes, trade and business secrets, know-how and good-will."*

Sources: [(UNCTAD Navigator, China-Germany BIT 2003)](https://investmentpolicy.unctad.org/international-investment-agreements/treaties/bit/905/china---germany-bit-2003-); [(EDIT/WTI, China-Germany BIT 2003)](https://edit.wti.org/document/show/8999e77e-918a-48d4-a598-cee246021534); [(italaw, China-Germany BIT 2003)](https://www.italaw.com/investment-treaties/china-germany-bit-2003)

**Calibration note:** The treaty PDF download returned 403; the provision text is sourced from three independent authoritative international law treaty databases reporting consistently. Confidence: medium-high.

**Significance:** The Germany-China BIT goes beyond the China-Switzerland BIT (2009) (which covers only "know-how") by also explicitly naming **"trade and business secrets."** "Trade and business secrets" is the most direct treaty-text hook for the Ferguson/Kim pharmaceutical trade-secret scenario — clinical trial data characterised as proprietary secrets subject to confidentiality obligations, not merely as technical know-how. German pharmaceutical investors (Bayer, Merck KGaA, Boehringer Ingelheim) hold this treaty basis against China. The BIT entered into force 11 November 2005.

#### A.2 China-France BIT (2007) — "know-how"

EDIT/WTI and Jusmundi database references for the China-France BIT (2007) report that the investment definition includes intellectual property rights encompassing *"copyrights, patents, trademarks, trade names, know-how and goodwill."*

Sources: [(EDIT/WTI, China-France BIT 2007)](https://edit.wti.org/document/show/7d16a9bd-031d-4ae5-9fb5-c844a64c44e0); [(Jusmundi, China-France BIT 2007)](https://jusmundi.com/en/document/treaty/fr-accord-entre-le-gouvernement-de-la-republique-francaise-et-le-gouvernement-de-la-republique-populaire-de-chine-sur-lencouragement-et-la-protection-reciproques-des-investissements-china-france-bit-2007-monday-26th-november-2007)

**Calibration note:** Same 403 issue on direct text. Two treaty databases report "know-how" consistently. Confidence: medium.

#### A.3 Net T04 finding — European BIT architecture complete

All three major European pharmaceutical investor home states now have China BITs providing a treaty-text Ring 1 basis for pharmaceutical clinical trial data claims:

| BIT | In Force | IP Investment Definition | Best Treaty Hook |
|-----|----------|--------------------------|-----------------|
| China-Switzerland (2009) | Yes | "know-how" (confirmed from treaty text, Day 3) | know-how |
| China-Germany (2003) | Yes (from 11 Nov 2005) | "trade and business secrets" + "know-how" + "technical processes" | trade and business secrets |
| China-France (2007) | Yes | "know-how" | know-how |

The Germany-China BIT is the most directly applicable for the pharmaceutical trade-secret scenario. The EU-China CAI remains suspended as of June 2026 (European Parliament conditioned ratification on lifting of Chinese counter-sanctions from March 2021; no lifting has occurred), so European investors must rely on member-state BITs individually. Sources: [(European Parliament Legislative Train, EU-China CAI)](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/legislative-train/theme-a-global-europe-leveraging-our-power-and-partnerships/file-eu-china-investment-agreement)

### B. Thread T05 — UNCITRAL WG III: 59th Commission Session Begins in Three Days

#### B.1 The July 2026 milestone is live

The UNCITRAL 59th Commission session is scheduled for **June 29 – July 10, 2026** in New York — opening in three days from this meeting. Source: [(UNCITRAL, Sessions and Events)](https://uncitral.un.org/en/gateway/meetings)

#### B.2 The Commission deliverable: procedural Protocol only (A/CN.9/1246)

The key document is **A/CN.9/1246** (April 15, 2026): *"Possible reform of investor-State dispute settlement (ISDS): Draft supplementary provisions on the conduct of proceedings to resolve international investment disputes."* Its confirmed scope is entirely procedural: evidence, bifurcation, interim measures, manifest lack of legal merit, security for costs, third-party funding disclosure, counterclaims, and cost allocation. A companion document, A/CN.9/1269, compiles state comments on A/CN.9/1246. These are the ISDS reform items on the 59th Commission agenda. Sources: [(UNCITRAL, A/CN.9/1246)](https://uncitral.un.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/a_cn9_1246_advance_copy.pdf); [(UNCITRAL, Procedural Rules page)](https://uncitral.un.org/en/proceduralrulesreformandcrosscuttingissues)

#### B.3 DOB provision NOT in the July 2026 Commission package — confirmed by category

A/CN.9/1246's scope is procedural. The denial-of-benefits provision (Draft Provision 9 in WP.253, formerly Draft Provision 17 in WP.244) is a substantive investor-eligibility provision; it does not appear in A/CN.9/1246 and is outside its subject matter. The 54th WG III session (March 23–27, 2026, Vienna) focused on draft statutes for permanent first-tier and appellate tribunals — not on the DOB provision. Sources: [(IISD ITN, 54th session report, April 21 2026)](https://www.iisd.org/itn/2026/04/21/uncitral-working-group-iii-advances-isds-reform-but-progress-remains-slow/); [(UNCITRAL, 54th session overview, Indico)](https://indico.un.org/event/1022011/)

**Implication:** The natural-person nationality-planning gap from *Jason Yu Song v. China* (PCA 2019-39) is NOT addressed by the July 2026 Commission package. Ring 3 doctrine architecture remains unchanged. Next active WG III session: October 12–16, 2026 in Vienna (tentative).
