## 1.6 The honest form of a whole-history denominator

`git rev-list --all` is **not a history walk** in a shallow clone; it is a walk of whatever depth window the harness happened to cut, whose floor advances with the calendar. `0906-B10`'s countermeasure names a corpus that shrinks between sessions **and undercounts by a third within any one session.**

The honest form carries four parameters, and the third is the one that was missing:

> **N** distinct `(path, blob)` pairs under `<path>`, over `git rev-list <TIP>` × `git ls-tree -r`, at TIP `<sha>`, **in a clone whose `.git/shallow` was absent — verified by `git rev-parse --is-shallow-repository` returning `false` at the time of the count** — counted `<date>`, over `<named ref set>`.

Operationally: **`git fetch --unshallow` before any history-derived count, or say "over the N commits this clone holds from `<TIP>`" and never say "committed" or "ever."** The fourth parameter matters too and is weaker than it looks: origin's ref set is itself mutable, so even an unshallowed `--all` is a statement about what origin advertised on a date.
