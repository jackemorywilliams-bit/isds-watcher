A Quiet Week: Reconciling the Okuashvili Posture and a Still-Unconfirmed UNCITRAL Outcome

No screened item reached the IP/trade-secret intersection this week; the value lies in correcting an inverted procedural posture in Okuashvili and honestly flagging the UNCITRAL 59th-session outcome as unverified.

This week's items against the research question

Nothing screened this week reaches the three-ring intersection of IP-as-investment, regulatory/judicial measure, and the jurisdictional gate. The Okuashvili matter is a Ring-3-mechanics artefact only: it concerns tobacco-business assets and the forced sale thereof, with no patent, trademark, trade secret, or clinical-data nexus. Its relevance to the project is confined to its MFN-as-forum-substitution reasoning, which is a candidate doctrine-bank note rather than a development on the theme.

The transferable structural point is the effet-utile objection: applying an MFN provision to substitute the designated arbitral forum risks rendering meaningless the express choice between treaty arbitration-clause variants. That reasoning is paraphrased from the analyst's reading and the secondary coverage, not quoted from a verified primary instrument; it should be treated as illustrative of the abuse-of-right and critical-date logic in the Ring 3 line rather than as a logged holding.

The Court of Appeal of Santiago salmon award (headline only, holding not characterized) touches Ring 2 nominally via an ultra petita set-aside but carries no IP, standing, or abuse-of-right question. It is a genuine near-miss and is dropped from the theme.

Correcting an inverted procedural posture in Okuashvili

The screening layer appears to have inverted the procedural posture by reading an undated, paywalled IAReporter headline (suggesting a Swedish court blessed the MFN detour) alongside an earlier-instance document for the same case. The verifiable appellate record shows the opposite at the set-aside stage: the Svea Court of Appeal annulled the SCC tribunal's pro-jurisdiction award for want of MFN consent (late 2024; the exact date and docket number remain unverified pending a primary source). The court then granted a right of appeal to the Swedish Supreme Court, which has since granted leave.

The attribution details require caution. The characterization of the claimant as a dual UK–Georgia national barred from ICSID, and the 1991 Model UK BIT drafting reasoning, are stated in the memo without a quoted accessible source and read as reconstructed doctrine; they should be treated as unconfirmed paraphrase. Likewise, the SCC partial award date (2022, day-level precision unverified) and the named dissenter should be carried only as flagged-for-confirmation unless pinned to a source actually read.

The inference that the IAReporter headline reports a later Supreme Court merits ruling reversing the Svea Court is explicitly backsourced and unconfirmed. Do not log item 3 as a holding until a dated Supreme Court judgment is pinned to an accessible source.

Open threads status

T05 (UNCITRAL 59th session) is the priority but its outcome is unconfirmed. The pre-session posture is supported: a Procedural Protocol was expected to be adopted, with MIC/MIIR statutes advanced only as drafts. The advance document A/CN.9/1246 and the session agenda are cited by the analyst but their document number, date, and the agenda URL's resolution should be confirmed against a source actually retrieved before being relied upon. No post-session primary or secondary report confirming adoption was located, and the stated session-closing date is itself unverified; the analyst correctly declined to upgrade 'expected to be adopted' to 'adopted.'

T01 (Einarsson) saw no new development located and is unchanged but not affirmatively re-confirmed this week. T04 (Hela Schwarz / China data exclusivity) is unchanged, with no new filings or full-award access. T02/T03 show no movement; the trade-secret / clinical-data case still has not materialised and the Ferguson–Kim framework remains theoretical.

Net contribution: a methodological correction

The session's genuine value is methodological. It demonstrates that the screening layer can invert a procedural posture when it ingests an undated, paywalled headline alongside an earlier-instance document for the same case, potentially logging a holding and its opposite as if both were the tribunal's. The recommended fix is a posture-reconciliation check: any case touching more than one forum or instance must be reconciled by decision-date and instance before any holding is logged.

The Okuashvili MFN effet-utile reasoning is retained only as a flagged near-miss / candidate doctrine note. Its promotion to a contribution 'directly transferable' to a hypothetical trade-secret MFN claim is speculative and rests on the unconfirmed posture, so it is downgraded accordingly. The methodology point stands on its own as the week's compounding gain.
