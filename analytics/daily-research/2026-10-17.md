## 3. Priority 2 — ring 3 across the whole corpus

**Availability first, because it defeats the simple version of the question.**
`triage.strengths` exists on **148 of 488 rows only**. On 2026-08-17, 08-24, 08-31 and 09-07
(328 rows) `triage.basis` is `"not_run"` and `ran` is `false` on every row. For those four dates
ring 3 is **missing, not absent**, and nothing about it can be asserted.

**On the 148 rows where triage ran, ring 3 is the MOST-firing ring, not the dead one:**
non-absent **72 of 148** (`weak` 39, `present` 22, `strong` 11), against ring 2 at 52/148 and
ring 1 at **4/148**.

**In the derived `verdict_v2` layer it is the opposite,** and the loss is one-directional. Over
the 148 rows carrying both layers: ring 3 `triage` non-absent → `v2` absent in **62** rows;
reverse, 1. Ring 2: 40 and 2. Ring 1: 1 and 0. Corpus-wide, ring 3's `v2` basis is `lexical` on
463 of 477, `semantic` on 9, `semantic_unverified` on 5.

**And here is the hard finding. Ring 3 has never once been lexically PRESENT.** `src/classify.py`
sets `PRESENT_FLOOR = 12` and a ring is scored present only at or above it
(`present_rings = [r for r, s in per_ring_subtotal.items() if s >= PRESENT_FLOOR]`, line 433).
Measured over all 488 rows:

| ring | rows with nonzero subtotal | max observed | rows ≥ 12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ip_as_investment` | 23 | 12 | 3 |
| `judicial_or_regulatory_measure` | 17 | 14 | 7 |
| `jurisdictional_admissibility` | **5** | **9** | **0** |

Only **three distinct documents** in 488 rows ever produced a ring-3 lexical hit, and the five
occurrences are three repeats of one scholar row (`treaty_shopping`, subtotal 9) plus
`exhaustion_of_local_remedies` (3) and `denial_of_benefits` (2). Matching is exact substring
(`if phrase in haystack`, line 402) over 17 literal phrases. **`"abuse of right"` — weight 12,
the only ring-3 phrase that alone reaches the floor — has never matched once.** Nor have
`"abuse of process"` (9), `"corporate restructuring"` (8), `"reasonably foreseeable"` (7) or
`"critical date"` (6). Observed ceiling 9 < floor 12.

**Hypothesis separation.**
- **(a) a quiet world — weakened, not excluded.** The semantic triage pass credits ring-3
  content in 48.6% of the rows it read, including 11 `strong`. Whatever that is, it is not an
  instrument seeing nothing.
- **(b) ring-3 detectors mis-specified — SUPPORTED, and for a specific reason.** Ring 3's
  lexicon is award-paragraph doctrinal phrasing matched against candidate text whose median
  length is 312 chars on 2026-10-12 and 80–465 chars on six of eight run dates; 14 of 35 rows
  have `body_sha256 == title_sha256`. A headline does not contain "pre-existing or reasonably
  foreseeable dispute".
- **(c) ring 3 only ever gets a lexical basis — SUPPORTED, and the repo states the cause.**
  `src/config.py`: *"Until now it derived it from lexical subtotals only, because
  `prompts/classifier.txt` returns a bare ring list with no evidence spans, so every
  model-claimed ring failed verification by construction."* That closes the chain: legacy ring
  list has no spans → the evidence guard refuses it → strength falls back to lexical → ring-3
  lexical can never reach 12 → ring 3 absent. (b) and (c) are the explanation; (a) is not needed
  to account for the zero.

**What I cannot separate locally.** Whether the `v2` derivation *ought* to read
`triage.strengths` is a design question, not a measurement; and whether ring 3 would clear the
floor on full award text cannot be tested without a build (re-scoring a body corpus) — both
approval-gated. `guard_demoted` is true on only 5 of 477 ring-3 rows, so the loss is **not** the
guard firing; it is the fallback path, which is a quieter and worse failure.

**So: if the instrument cannot see ring 3, that is a better explanation than a quiet world.** On
the scoring limb I now think it is the better explanation. Nearest prior art named: the
2026-10-14 thread establishes ring 3's missing **retrieval** surface (the ICSID
tables-of-decisions registers). Today's is the **scoring** limb — the floor, not the feed — and
