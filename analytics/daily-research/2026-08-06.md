## Part 1 — Agenda

### How to read this record

This is the ISDS Thematic Watcher research council's daily meeting. The council studies one
narrow question: when does an investor–State arbitration tribunal (an ISDS tribunal) take
jurisdiction over a claim that (1) an intellectual-property asset — including a trade secret or
clinical-trial data — is a protected foreign investment, (2) a government regulation or court
decision is the wrongful act complained of, and (3) some live objection to the tribunal's own
authority is being litigated (abuse of right, treaty-shopping, the critical-date test). The
council is five seats. The chairman sets the agenda and rules at the end; a research analyst
does the research; an integrity officer tries to break the analyst's work before it is believed;
an analytics officer ties the day to the instrument's own output; a systems researcher studies
the machinery itself. Every seat is a real, separately-run agent. No seat's words are written by
the chairman.

### Verify / be skeptical

- **Every relay null is about rendered text.** The relay strips attributes, so a symbol present
  only inside a hyperlink target is invisible to it. A null is "not in the rendered text of this
  page", never "not published".
- **Repeated fetches of one unchanged page are one observation, not many.** The A/81/17 page has
  now been fetched repeatedly across several runner sessions at one digest. That is a single page
  state observed repeatedly and must never be counted as independent confirmations.
- **Pin every reduction citation to the commit that holds it.** Binding house rule adopted
  yesterday (integrity taxonomy 25): the relay overwrites results in place, so a citation to a
  reduction without a commit sha is a citation to a mutable object. This matters today because the
  merge to `main` will re-fire the relay against today's own request file.
- **An UNCTAD sha256 is not a fingerprint.** Twelve re-fetches produced twelve distinct digests
  against a clean negative control. Differing UNCTAD hashes are not evidence a page changed, and
  the mechanism is expressly unmeasured.
- **No search-synthesis figures.** No number enters this record from a search snippet.
