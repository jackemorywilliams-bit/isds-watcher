### (a) PROPOSED CLOSURE CONDITION

**CLOSE-POSITIVE.** The thread closes affirmatively when a session records, **from the body of a primary document** — a tribunal decision or award, or an ICSID/PCA docket entry naming that document — that a tribunal has disposed of a claim under a codified manifest-lack-of-merit gate (ICSID Convention Arbitration Rule 41; ICSID Additional Facility Arbitration Rule 51(1); ICC Rules Art. 30; the UNCITRAL Supplementary Provisions early-dismissal track) **where the stated ground of the disposition is whether an intellectual-property right — patent, trademark, copyright, geographical indication, trade secret, or clinical-trial data — is or is not a covered investment.** Two elements are required and both must be present in the document: **(i)** the instrument, named; **(ii)** the ground, IP-as-covered-investment.

**Checkable in one command or one query.** The standing observation is ICSID's own *Decisions on Manifest Lack of Legal Merit* table at `https://icsid.worldbank.org/cases/content/tables-of-decisions/manifest-lack-of-legal-merit` — a host on `ALLOWED_HOSTS` (`scripts/fetch_relay.py:49`), so **one relay row per cycle checks the whole population**, against the officer-verified 2026-07-30 baseline. The web-search complement is one query per cycle pairing a gate name with an IP term.

**What the condition does NOT license.**
1. **It does not close on a secondary source.** An ITN, Jus Mundi, Daily Jus or law-firm write-up is a LEAD. The record's own rule — a tribunal holding may only cite a primary source — governs.
2. **It does not close on an abuse-of-right, treaty-shopping, or critical-date admissibility disposition**, however IP-heavy the underlying investment. That is the *Philip Morris* line and a different thread; closing there closes the wrong thread.
3. **It does not close on a claimant's pleading.** That IP was *asserted* among the investments is not a disposition *touching* IP-as-investment. The tribunal must have ruled on the IP characterisation.
4. **It does not close on a title, a docket caption, or a URL path segment.**

**THIRD BUCKET — and today's searches are why it is needed.** The thread poses a binary: IP-as-investment *versus* abuse-of-right admissibility. The observable universe contains at least a third class — **a gate firing on a ground that is neither, over an investment in which IP was one asserted component** (territoriality, nationality, *ratione temporis*). A disposition of that kind must be logged to a subsidiary register, `gate fired / IP asserted / non-IP ground`, and **must not close the thread**. Without this bucket the first real instance will be mis-filed in whichever direction the reader is predisposed toward.

**STRUCTURALLY-UNREACHABLE CONDITION.** The thread is declared unreachable by this instrument when **either** (a) each named gate's own text, read from a primary source, excludes treaty-based investor-State arbitration from the gate's scope, so no such disposition can arise under it — for ICC Art. 30 this is precisely the question raised and left unsettled below; **or** (b) the surfaces that would record such a disposition are off the relay allowlist and fetch-dead across **three consecutive sessions in which a batch was actually commissioned against them**.
