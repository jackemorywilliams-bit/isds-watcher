### 4.5 ATTACK ON MY OWN STRONGEST ASSERTION

The "0 of 233" is the strongest thing in this memo and it has four weaknesses, which I state rather than let the chairman find:

1. **It is a screen on URL slugs, not on headlines.** Slugs are headline-derived but lossy. Measured: slug length min 31, max 199, mean 117.2 characters, with **5 of 233 sitting exactly at the 199-character cap** and therefore truncated (one visibly ends mid-word at `...failure-to-state-rea`). ### 4.7 Method note for self-training

Today's productive formulation was **title-as-query**: the full IAReporter headline typed as a search string (S2) returned, in one call, the UNCTAD case locator, the GAR headline and the Kluwer title — the entire backsource chain. Next sitting: fire the title verbatim, then spend the second call on a *different document class* (a docket page, a firm note, an ITN entry) rather than a refinement of the same query.
